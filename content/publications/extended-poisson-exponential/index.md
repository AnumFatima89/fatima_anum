abstract: |
  A new mixture of Modified Exponential (ME) and Poisson distribution has been introduced in this paper. 
  Taking the Maximum of Modified Exponential random variable when the sample size follows a zero truncated Poisson 
  distribution we have derived the new distribution, named as Extended Poisson Exponential distribution. 
  This distribution possesses increasing and decreasing failure rates. The Poisson-Exponential, Modified Exponential 
  and Exponential distributions are special cases of this distribution. We have also investigated some mathematical 
  properties of the distribution along with Information entropies and Order statistics of the distribution. 
  The estimation of parameters has been obtained using the Maximum Likelihood Estimation procedure. 
  Finally we have illustrated real data applications of our distribution.
