Our paper, **“A Pure Hypothesis Test for Inhomogeneous Random Graph Models Based on a Kernelised Stein Discrepancy,”** with **Gesine Reinert**, was published in the **Proceedings of the 29th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Statistics (AISTATS 2026)**.

The paper introduces a kernelised Stein discrepancy-based test for assessing the fit of inhomogeneous random graph models using a single observed network.

The paper appears in **Proceedings of Machine Learning Research (PMLR), Volume 300, pp. 1225–1233**.
