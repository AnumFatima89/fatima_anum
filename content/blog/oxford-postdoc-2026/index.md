I am delighted to have joined the **Department of Statistics at the University of Oxford** as a **Postdoctoral Research Associate in Statistics**.

My research focuses on **uncertainty quantification in AI methods**, with current work on auditing and developing differentially private graph generators.

I am working as part of the **EPSRC–Turing FAIR programme (Framework for responsible adoption of Artificial Intelligence in the financial seRvices industry)** under the supervision of **Professor Gesine Reinert**, in collaboration with **Dr Andrew Elliott at the University of Glasgow**.
