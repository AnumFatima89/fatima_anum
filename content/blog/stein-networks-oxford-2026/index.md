I was pleased to present my work, **“A Pure Hypothesis Test for Inhomogeneous Random Graph Models Based on a Kernelised Stein Discrepancy,”** at the **Stein's Method and Networks** workshop at the **University of Oxford** on 1 July 2026.

The talk focused on a kernelised Stein discrepancy-based hypothesis test for assessing the fit of inhomogeneous random graph models using a single observed network.
