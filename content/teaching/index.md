**2023–2024**

I delivered tutorial sessions for *Probability and Statistics for Network Analysis* to groups of up to 13 students. I assessed student progress and provided end-of-term reports through the Oxford Teaching Management System.

### Graduate Teaching Assistant
**2021–2023**

I worked as a Graduate Teaching Assistant for:

- *Probability and Statistics for Network Analysis*

My responsibilities included marking problem sheets, providing constructive feedback, maintaining attendance and assessment records, and demonstrating solutions during tutorial sessions.
